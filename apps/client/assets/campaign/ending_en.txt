### EPILOGUE
# THE FALL OF THE SHOGUN



### YEAR 2172
## THE LAST BATTLE

In the orbit of Saturn, the Bydoo Shogun has fallen.

Alone against it, James Cooper held on until his last ship. Then the R-Type Section came back for him, and together its pilots brought down the weapon humanity had built.

Bereft of their Shogun, the Bydoo fall back to the edges of the galaxy.



### YEAR 2173
## A FRAGILE PEACE

For the first time in decades, the space lanes are safe again.

The colonies rebuild, and humanity takes up the exploration of the stars once more.

But somewhere in the dark, the Bydoo keep evolving.

The R-Type Section stays on alert.



# THE END

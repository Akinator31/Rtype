### TIMELINE
# THE ERA OF CONFLICT



### YEAR 2126
## THE CONQUEST OF SPACE

Humanity takes a major step in its evolution by mastering space travel.

Over the years, mankind explores the galaxy in search of new planets to colonise, of resources still unknown and of alien civilisations to discover. The horizon of humanity no longer knows any limit.



### YEAR 2148
## THE FIRST SIGNAL

Space exploration bears fruit. Humanity discovers new sources of energy and resources never suspected before. Yet, despite years of exploration, no intelligent life has been found.

That same year, a mysterious coded signal is intercepted. Its origin remains unknown and its content incomprehensible. Scientists begin long research to decipher the message.



### YEAR 2156
## THE WARNING

Space travel is now part of everyday life for humanity. After years of research, the signal intercepted in 2148 is finally deciphered.

But its content is far from reassuring.

The message seems to warn humanity that it has just crossed a threshold it should never have reached. A confrontation is now inevitable and imminent.

Facing this unknown threat, the great powers begin to build ever more sophisticated warships, preparing for a conflict they still know nothing about.



### YEAR 2163
## FIRST CONTACT

The warning comes true. Humanity makes contact with a hostile alien civilisation: the Bydoo.

Their technology far exceeds that of humans and their military superiority proves overwhelming. Battles multiply and the human forces suffer heavy defeats.

Against such an advanced enemy, humanity seems doomed. Its very survival is now at stake.



### YEAR 2166
## THE SHOGUN PROJECT

Human losses are tremendous. Yet a new hope appears: human fighters manage to capture a Bydoo.

This capture marks a turning point in the war. After long research and many experiments, scientists manage to understand and master part of the alien technology.

Humanity then sets out to merge its own technological advances with those of the Bydoo, to design an arsenal like no other.

In this context the Shogun project is born, an ambitious programme meant to put an end to human losses and to turn the tide of the war for good.

Meanwhile, a training programme for elite pilots is launched. The best of them are selected to join the R-Type section, an elite unit specialised in space combat.



### YEAR 2170
## THE TURNING OF THE TIDE

Thanks to the new technologies, humanity finally regains the upper hand over the Bydoo.

But this victory is short-lived.

The alien civilisation evolves in turn and develops a particularly dangerous ability: taking control of human ships.

The battles become far more even. Neither side now seems able to gain a decisive advantage.

Meanwhile, the Shogun project nears completion. However, a growing concern spreads among the leaders of the programme: its power could become uncontrollable and turn against its own creators.

Facing this risk, humanity begins to hesitate over the future of the project.



### YEAR 2172
## THE SHOGUN THREAT

The fears of humanity come true.

After a lost battle, the Bydoo seize control of the Shogun project and unleash it across the galaxy.

The situation becomes critical. A new threat adds to that of the Bydoo, and the project meant to save humanity is now one of its greatest dangers.

Fortunately, the R-Type section has made great progress. Through intensive training and the mastery of the new technologies, its pilots are now able to defeat the Bydoo in most of their battles.

The war finally seems to turn in favour of humanity.

Only one major obstacle remains: the Bydoo Shogun.

As long as this threat survives, no lasting peace can be considered.

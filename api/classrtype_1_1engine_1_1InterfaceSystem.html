Detailed Description

Lets the player use the widgets of the scene that receives the input: focus, navigation with the keyboard, a gamepad or the mouse, and activation.

  • Up and Down move the focus by Focusable order, around at the ends; the mouse focuses the widget under it when it moves.
  • Confirm activates the focused widget, Left and Right change its value, a click activates the widget under the pointer, the wheel changes its value.
  • Back goes back from the scene (Scenes::back()).
  • The focused widget bounces to InterfaceStyle::focusScale, and goes back to its size when it loses the focus. A console text field keeps its size. A widget with a Panel (a button) keeps its size too: its panel lights up instead, fading to the focused look while the pointer is over it, or while it has the focus once the keyboard or a gamepad is used.

The actions of the widgets run after the update (Ecs::defer()): a button may open a menu, which creates entities while this system is still going through the widgets.

Register it right after the Scenes, before the game systems: the game checks Scenes::receivesInput() for its own scene, so it does not react to keys used by a menu.

Detailed Description

A ship of the menu going back and forth between its dock on the station and the space out of the screen.

The FlightSystem moves its Model along the route of the stage: nose forward while it flies, along heading while it goes down, stays and goes up. Each stage plays its animation on the ModelAnimation of the entity, if it has one. Each time the ship comes back, it is another of models, one that no other ship flies.

A route is a curve from from to to, pulled towards control: the ship leaves from heading towards control, and reaches to coming from it.

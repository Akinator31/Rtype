Detailed Description

The hits of the game.

A Bydo shot hurts the ships: a ship that is hit is destroyed, its player loses a life, and gets a new ship after Rules::respawnDelay ticks, invulnerable for Rules::invulnerableTicks, until it has no life left. Once every player is out of lives, the game is over (GameOutcome::Defeat). The entities are destroyed after the systems of the tick.

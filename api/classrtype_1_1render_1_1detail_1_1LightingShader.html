Detailed Description

Owns the lighting shader of the 3D models: a directional light, such as a sun, plus an ambient light, with the normal map of the material when it has one, and its emission map, which glows whatever the light.

If the shader cannot be compiled, a warning is logged and models are drawn unlit, with the colours of their textures.

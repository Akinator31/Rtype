Detailed Description

The lobby of the master: the TLS connections of the clients, and the requests they send (docs/protocol/lobby.md), on the thread of the loop.

rtype::server::Lobby lobby(loop, std::move(listener), identity, games);
// ...
lobby.close(); // Ctrl+C: the clients are disconnected

  • Each connection goes through the states of the protocol: TLS handshake (10 s), then HELLO (10 s), then the lobby and the games, closed after 30 s of silence. Each state accepts its own requests; the others get the ERROR the protocol gives (HELLO_REQUIRED, ALREADY_WELCOMED, ALREADY_IN_GAME, NOT_IN_GAME, UNKNOWN_MESSAGE).
  • Every request gets exactly one reply, in order: the requests that follow a CREATE_GAME wait for its game. A malformed one gets ERROR (MALFORMED), then the connection is closed, as after ERROR (UNSUPPORTED_VERSION).
  • HELLO gives the player an id, never 0 and never given twice by this master.
  • The games are those of Games: LIST_GAMES, CREATE_GAME, JOIN_GAME and LEAVE_GAME go to it, and a player who leaves its game without asking (game over, crash, timeout) is told with LEFT_GAME.

Detailed Description

Places the entity on the screen: its own point point is put on the same point of the screen, then moved by the offset.

With TopLeft and an offset of {20, 20}, the top left corner of the entity is 20 units from the top left corner of the screen; with BottomRight, use {-20, -20}. Computed by the AnchorSystem from the size of what the entity draws, so the entity must not have a Parent.

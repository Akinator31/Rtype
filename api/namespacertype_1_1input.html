◆ Key

enum rtype::input::Key
strong

A keyboard key, named after its position on a US (QWERTY) keyboard.

Keys are physical positions, not characters: Key::W is the key left of E, which is labelled Z on a French (AZERTY) keyboard. Default controls such as WASD therefore become ZQSD on AZERTY without any change.

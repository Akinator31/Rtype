Detailed Description

Draws the entities with the Renderer: those with a Drawable, a Position and a Text, Sprite, Box, Panel or Toast, and the 3D models (Drawable and Model) seen through the Camera, before the rest of the World layer.

A Backdrop is drawn first, behind the models.

It is not a system of the Ecs: drawing happens between Renderer::beginFrame() and endFrame(), after the update, and may happen several times per frame (once per eye in VR). Each layer is drawn separately; within a layer, entities are drawn by Drawable order, then by id.

Hidden entities (see ecs::isHidden()) and entities of hidden scenes are skipped, and so are the entities of the interface layer under a scene that hides the interface below (SceneSettings::hidesInterfaceBelow). The focused widget gets a frame around its Hitbox and the focused colour, unless it has a Panel, which shows the focus itself; a focused text field also shows its selection and cursor.

Detailed Description

Look and timing of the toasts (see Engine::showToast()): a dark rounded panel with a coloured accent on its left, then the text on one line, written with the font of the buttons.

Toasts are stacked from a corner of the screen, the newest nearest to it. Sizes are in game units, durations in seconds.

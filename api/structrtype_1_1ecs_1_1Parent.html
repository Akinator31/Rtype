Detailed Description

Attaches an entity to another one, its parent: the Force to the ship, a turret to a boss, a button to its panel.

The child moves with its parent (see TransformSystem), is hidden with it (see isHidden()), and is destroyed with it (see Ecs::destroyEntity()).

Detailed Description

Small vector maths for placing and flying models in the 3D world of the menu.

Enumeration Type Documentation

◆ FlightStage

enum rtype::client::FlightStage
strong

What a ship of the menu is doing. The stages follow each other in this order, then start again.

Enumerator
Approach 

Flies from out of the screen to above its dock, slowing down.

Landing 

Goes straight down onto its dock.

Docked 

Stays on its dock.

TakeOff 

Goes straight up from its dock.

Departure 

Flies from above its dock out of the screen, faster and faster.

Away 

Waits out of the screen before coming back.

◆ buildMainMenu()

void rtype::client::buildMainMenu ( engine::Engine &  engine,
engine::SceneId  scene,
render::ResourceManager &  resources,
const engine::InterfaceStyle &  style,
const std::function< void()> &  quit 
)

Fills the main menu: a desert planet on the left, seen from orbit, a space station far on the right with ships landing on it and leaving, and a column of buttons in the bottom left corner: Play, Online and Settings, which open their submenu (see createSubmenuScenes()), and Quit.

The ships need a FlightSystem. The scene must keep updating when covered, so that the ships keep flying behind the submenus.

Parameters
resourcesWhere the models are loaded from. It must outlive the scene.
styleLook of the buttons. It must outlive the scene.
quitCalled when the player presses the quit button.

Declares the submenus of the main menu.

Each one opens over the main menu, whose world stays visible but not its buttons, and shows its title after a back button that closes it, like Escape. Play offers a game offline; the content of Settings is to come. The Online menu is declared by OnlineScenes.

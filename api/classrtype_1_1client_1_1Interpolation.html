Detailed Description

Draws a world received in snapshots smoothly (docs/protocol/game.md, section 9.3, "Drawing"): the snapshots come 30 times per second, late or lost sometimes, but the client draws at its frame rate.

It keeps the last worlds received, each with its tick, and draws the world a little in the past, at the drawn tick: Delay behind the newest world, between the two worlds around it.

interpolation.receive(world.tick(), entities, players); // a snapshot applied
interpolation.destroyed(id, tick); // ENTITIES_DESTROYED
interpolation.advance(elapsed); // each frame
const rtype::game::Snapshot drawn = interpolation.view();
rtype::game::Snapshot
The world at the end of a tick, as the game sees it: what the client draws, offline (LocalSession) or...
Definition: Snapshot.hpp:92
  • The drawn tick advances with the time of the frames, 60 ticks per second, and is nudged towards its target (the newest tick minus Delay): a little faster when it falls behind, a little slower when it gets ahead, never backwards. The jitter of the snapshots and the drift between the clocks are absorbed without a jump; after a pause, too far behind, it jumps to its target.
  • An entity is drawn between its positions in the two worlds around the drawn tick. Past the newest world (snapshots late or lost), it goes on with its velocity for MaxExtrapolation at most, then waits.
  • It appears once the drawn tick reaches its first world, and disappears once it reaches its destruction: not 100 ms early, when the message arrives.

Pure: no clock, no network. The time is given by advance().

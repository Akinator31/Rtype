Detailed Description

Shows the toasts (see Engine::showToast()), with the look and timing of InterfaceStyle::toast: stacks them from the corner of the style, the newest nearest to it, fades and slides them in and out, and destroys them once faded out.

When there are more than ToastStyle::maxVisible, the oldest ones fade out early. A new toast takes its place at once; the others glide to theirs.

It writes the Position, Opacity and Drawable of each toast, and its width from its text. Register it before the TransformSystem.

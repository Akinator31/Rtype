Detailed Description

Plays the intro of the main menu: waits until the player presses any key, gamepad button or mouse button while the scene of the Intro receives the input, then flies its Camera from the asteroids to the view of the menu, shows the Hidden elements with an IntroSlide and moves them all to their place.

Register it before the AnchorSystem, which places the elements from their Anchor.
